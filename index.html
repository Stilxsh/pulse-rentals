Frequently Asked Questions

Once your booking is confirmed, a Pulse Rentals driver delivers the vehicle directly to your residence, workplace, or airport terminal. We perform a quick walkaround inspection with you, hand over the keys, and you are ready to drive.
Drivers must be 21+ for executive hatchbacks (e.g., Mercedes A-Class) and 25+ for performance vehicles (e.g., RSQ3, Range Rover SVR) with a valid full driver’s license held for at least 2 years.
A pre-authorization hold or deposit is placed prior to delivery. Once the vehicle is returned in the same condition with no traffic violations, the deposit hold is released immediately.
